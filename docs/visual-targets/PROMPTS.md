# Visual targets: prompts and how to read them

**These are design targets, not screenshots of the implemented game.** They show the camera, HUD layout, colour and mood to build toward. Compare real captures against them in `docs/VISUAL_REVIEW.md`.

- **Route:** A, ChatGPT image generation (gpt-image) in the user's own ChatGPT account in Chrome. Route B (imagegen CLI) was not available because `OPENAI_API_KEY` is not set.
- **Chat:** https://chatgpt.com/c/6ae71b8d-ba84-83eb-a428-7ae5842abd4d (one chat, five turns, each turn builds on the previous image's style).
- **Date:** 2026-11-01.
- **Files:** ChatGPT delivered 1672×941 PNGs of 2.2–2.6 MB. They were converted to JPEG at quality 92 (`sips -s format jpeg -s formatOptions 92`) to stay under 2 MB: 0.55–0.73 MB each. The original PNGs are in `assets-src/mockups-raw/`, which is git-ignored.
- **Attempts:** each image is the first generation from its prompt. None needed a retry.
- **Hebrew text:** generated Hebrew is often wrong or misspelled. Copy the layout, sizes and hierarchy, not the text. The real strings come from the game's own copy. In this set the Hebrew came out mostly correct, but check every string against `GAME_IDENTITY.md` anyway.
- **Physics in the images:** the images invent geography and scale (planet sizes, distances, the order of nodes). Never take a number or a planet position from a mockup. Physics, Sun size, gauges and planets are procedural (see `GAME_IDENTITY.md`).

## Shared HUD language (all gameplay shots)

| Element | Where | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Pause, mute | top-left | two round deep-navy buttons with a thin light ring and white icons |
| Level card | top-right | deep-navy rounded rectangle with a thin gold border. White or yellow bold Hebrew title on top. Under it: a parchment atlas-page icon and three stars (outlined = not earned, solid gold = earned). |
| Jump gauge "מד הקפיצה" | left edge, upper | tall narrow navy capsule. Label at the top, a vertical yellow fill tube with tick marks, a small Earth globe icon at the bottom of the tube (the Earth tick), and a yellow value under it ("פי 6", or "?" in transfer levels) |
| Thermometer "מד חום" | left edge, under the jump gauge | navy capsule, flame icon at the top, red→orange→blue gradient tube, snowflake at the bottom, white needle tab on the right of the tube |
| Objective | bottom-centre | navy pill with a gold border, a yellow up-arrow icon on the right (RTL start), one line of white text |
| Fail / info caption | mid-screen | slightly tilted navy card with a gold border, two lines of yellow-white text |

Font: rounded bold sans (Rubik). Palette: deep navy `#13204a`-ish cards, warm yellow `#ffc928`-ish accents, white text. Cards are semi-opaque, not glassy.

## 01-first-30s.jpg: Moon tutorial

**How to read it.** Third-person camera low and behind, slightly below Nuri's jump apex, so Nuri is seen from behind and below, mid-leap, in the centre-right third of the frame at about a quarter of the screen height. Dust puffs hang in the air below Nuri (no wind). Earth is huge on the left and the small white Sun is top-centre-right; both are in a pitch-black, star-filled sky. A trail of glowing gold star crumbs arcs from Nuri up to a glowing parchment page on the crater rim cliff at the right. The ground is mid-grey regolith with craters. The image does not show the planetoid's horizon curving; the real game should, because the Moon is small. HUD: title card "ירח הקפיצות" with 1 of 3 stars; the jump gauge is almost full with the Earth icon at the bottom and "פי 6"; objective "קפצו אל הדף שעל שפת המכתש". Light: cool white key from the Sun, cyan-white rim light on Nuri's suit and helmet.

## 02-core-mercury.jpg: Mercury twilight band

**How to read it.** Camera low behind Nuri, who runs away from the camera, centred, along a glowing orange twilight strip that runs from the bottom-centre up to the horizon. On the left is the night side: blue frost, ice crystals, deep blue shadow. On the right is the day side: glowing orange-white rock, heat haze. A huge blazing Sun fills the upper-right corner, much bigger than on the Moon shot. Boulders on the day side cast long dark shadows towards the camera; those are the shade pockets. Smoke puffs trail from Nuri's boots. Star crumbs line the band, and a checkpoint flag with a ring stands ahead on the horizon. HUD: title "כספית – צד יום וצד לילה" with 2 of 3 stars; the jump gauge is about two-thirds full with "פי 2.6"; the thermometer sits under it with the needle high (hot); objective "רוצו בפס הדמדומים והסתתרו בצל". The terminator in the real game is computed from the Sun direction. The image's frost-versus-fire split is the colour target for the two sides.

## 04-level-select.jpg: level select / progression

**How to read it.** A 3/4 high view of a toy solar system across a starry background, laid out in RTL: the Sun is a big glowing orange arc on the right edge, and the path runs right to left from the rocky worlds near the Sun, past Earth in the centre, to the giants and a galaxy swirl at the top-left. A dotted gold path links round navy number badges, each with a 3-star strip under it. Earned stars are solid gold. The current node (8, on Earth) glows cyan, and Nuri stands on Earth waving. Locked nodes (16–18) are grey with a padlock. Top bar, right to left: an atlas counter card "דפי אטלס 7/18 ★ 19/54", then 4 world tabs: "העולמות הסלעיים" (selected, yellow fill), "כדור הארץ", "הענקים", "אל היקום". Each tab has a small planet icon. Bottom-left is a big yellow "שחקו ▶" button. Bottom-right is a round navy atlas-book button "אטלס". שביטון peeks in at top-centre-right, holding a stolen page. An asteroid-belt band runs across the bottom-left foreground. **What's off:** the node numbering and positions are scrambled (5 on Jupiter, 9–15 among the giants, 1–4 on small bodies between Earth and the Sun). Build the real order from `LEVELS.md`: World 1 (levels 1–5) next to the Sun, then Earth (6–9), the giants (10–14) and beyond (15–18). Take the visual language from the image, not the order.
